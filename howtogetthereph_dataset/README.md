# r/howtogetthereph — commute dataset

Six months of the Philippine commuting subreddit, pulled and cleaned.
**Coverage: 9 April 2026 → 9 October 2026.**

## What's in here

| file | rows/records | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `routes.csv` | 8,556 rows | every post that got a real answer — one row per route, with the top answer |
| `routes.json` | 16,387 records | every post, with its answers attached (up to 12 each) |
| `DIGEST.md` | — | human-readable: every answered post, question then answers |
| `ncr_routes.csv` | 3,480 rows | NCR→NCR only, one row per route |
| `ncr_routes.json` | 3,480 records | NCR→NCR only, with all answers |
| `ncr_DIGEST.md` | — | human-readable NCR-only |

## Numbers

- 16,387 posts · 39,869 comments (same date range)
- 8,556 posts answered (52%) · 15,395 answers total
- 3,480 NCR→NCR posts, 2,186 of them answered, 3,989 answers
- 840 NCR posts carry 2+ answers (the multi-option routes)

Title composition: 35% cross the NCR boundary · 21% NCR→NCR · 19% neither side NCR ·
12% prose rather than places · 10% a side too long to be a location.
