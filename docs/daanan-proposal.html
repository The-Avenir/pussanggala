Project proposal · Draft v0.1 · October 2026

Daanan: local-AI commute directions for the Philippines

A phone app that answers "Paano pumunta sa…?" the way a helpful local would: which jeep to ride, what the signboard says, where to get off and how much to pay. It runs on the device and works on weak or no mobile data.

Working name Daanan ("pathway") Team appBuilders Platforms Android first, then iOS Status Seeking approval for a 6-week prototype
Summary

The idea in one paragraph

Filipino commuters already solve this problem by asking each other. r/HowToGetTherePH has about 297,000 members posting and answering route questions, many of them outside Metro Manila. Daanan turns that kind of local, informal-transit knowledge into a structured route database. A small language model on the phone then answers questions in Taglish from that database. Unlike existing apps, it covers jeepneys, UV Express, tricycles and provincial buses outside NCR, and it keeps working when the signal drops.

"Pano pumunta sa SM Masinag galing Cubao? First time ko po."

  1. JeepRide a jeep with signboard Cubao – Antipolo along Aurora Blvd. Get off at SM Masinag, Marcos Hwy.≈ ₱20–30
  2. WalkThe mall entrance faces the highway. About 2 minutes.—

Example only. Fares and routes are illustrative. In the app, each step cites its source answers and shows when they were last confirmed.

Problem

Informal transit is mostly missing from route apps

  • The knowledge is tacit. Signboard text, unofficial stops, "baba sa may Jollibee" landmarks and current fares live in people's heads and in forum threads, not in GTFS feeds.
  • Coverage stops at NCR. Sakay.ph is strong in Metro Manila but does not cover other cities. Google Maps is weak on jeepneys and tricycles.
  • Asking takes hours. A Reddit question can take hours to get a good answer, and the answer gets buried. The same corridors are asked about again and again.
  • Data is unreliable on the road. Commuters hit dead zones, ration prepaid data, or run phones low on battery. Cloud-only AI fails exactly when they need it.

Solution

What Daanan does

Ask in plain Taglish

Type or speak a question. The model works out origin, destination and preferences such as cheapest, fewest transfers or avoiding EDSA.

Get step-by-step legs

Each leg shows the vehicle type, signboard, where to board and alight, a fare range, and a walking map from OSM data.

See where it came from

Each step links to the community answers behind it, with a "last confirmed" date and a confidence level.

Works offline

Region packs bundle map tiles, the route database and the model. You download Region IV-A once and use it without data.

Contribute routes

Commuters confirm, correct or add legs after a trip. This replaces scraping over time.

Private by design

Questions and location stay on the phone. Sharing contributions is opt-in and anonymised.

Architecture

How it works

The language model never invents geography. Retrieval supplies the routes and the map engine supplies distances. The model's job is to understand the question and write the answer from the retrieved legs.

Data strategy

Where the route knowledge comes from

Each route leg is stored as a structured record:

FieldExample
modejeepney · uv_express · bus · tricycle · rail · ferry · walk
signboard"Cubao – Antipolo"
board_at / alight_atLandmark text + coordinates, e.g. "Aurora Blvd cor. 15th Ave"
fare_min / fare_max₱ range, with the year it was reported
regionNCR · Region III · Region IV-A · …
sources / last_confirmedSource IDs, date, number of confirmations
notes"Rush hour: line at terminal 30+ min"

Reddit content needs a licensing decision before anything ships. Reddit's terms restrict scraping and commercial or AI use of its content. For the prototype, we use only a small set of hand-collected examples for internal testing. Before launch, we pick one of three paths: (a) a data agreement with Reddit, (b) asking moderators and contributors for permission and linking out instead of copying, or (c) building our own dataset from scratch through contributions and partners.

  • Seed sources we can use: OpenStreetMap public-transport relations, LTFRB published route and fare data where available, and partner LGUs or schools.
  • Contributions: after each trip, ask "Did this route work?" with one tap. Corrections go to a review queue.
  • Freshness: confidence drops as data ages and rises with confirmations. Fares older than 12 months are flagged in the app.
MVP scope

What the prototype includes

In scope

  • One corridor: Metro Manila ↔ Rizal (Cubao, Antipolo, Marikina, Cainta)
  • About 300 hand-checked route legs
  • Offline question answering on one mid-range Android phone
  • Answers with sources, fare ranges and a confidence level
  • Confirm or correct a route

Not in this phase

  • Live traffic or vehicle tracking
  • iOS app
  • Bulk ingestion from Reddit
  • Voice input
  • Turn-by-turn navigation while walking
Roadmap

Phases

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Data and extraction

    Define the leg schema. Hand-collect the Rizal corridor. Test LLM extraction against hand-labelled answers until it reaches at least 85% field accuracy.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Desktop prototype

    Build the retrieval, leg-stitching and answer pipeline on a laptop with Ollama. Write 100 test questions and score the answers.

  3. Weeks 5–6

    Android prototype

    Port to llama.cpp on Android with an offline region pack. Measure speed, RAM and battery use. Test with 10–15 commuters.

  4. Months 2–4

    Pilot

    Settle the data licensing path. Add contributions and the review queue. Expand to Region III and IV-A. Closed beta with about 500 users.

  5. Months 5+

    Public launch

    Add Visayas and Mindanao city packs (Cebu, Davao), an iOS app and voice input. Explore partnerships with LGUs and campuses.

Risks

Risks and mitigations

RiskLevelMitigation
Data licensing: Reddit terms block use of its contentHighDon't depend on it. Plan for contributions and open data from the start, and treat Reddit as a partnership option rather than the foundation.
A wrong route strands someoneHighAnswer only from retrieved legs. Show sources and confidence. Say "not enough data yet" instead of guessing.
Routes and fares go out of date (jeepney modernization, fare changes)MediumConfidence that drops with age, one-tap confirmations, and ingesting LTFRB fare announcements.
Small models struggle on low-end phonesMediumA 1.5B fallback model, template answers when no LLM is available, and an optional cloud mode.
Thin coverage at launch (cold start)MediumLaunch corridor by corridor. Recruit campus and office commuter groups as early contributors.
Region packs are large to downloadMediumSplit packs by province, offer Wi-Fi-only downloads, and share the model file across regions.

Resources

What the prototype needs

  • People: 1 ML / backend developer, 1 Android developer, and 1 part-time data curator who commutes the corridor.
  • Hardware: a laptop with a GPU (8 GB VRAM or more) for extraction and testing, plus two Android test phones (one low-end, one mid-range).
  • Software: all open source (llama.cpp, Ollama, SQLite, GraphHopper, OSM data). Cloud costs are limited to an optional extraction server. Check provider pricing calculators for estimates.
  • Time: 6 weeks to a go/no-go decision for the pilot.
Open questions

Decisions needed from the team

  • Which data path to pursue first: a Reddit agreement, moderator collaboration, or our own community?
  • Is the Rizal corridor the right first test, or should we start with a region Sakay.ph doesn't cover, such as Cebu?
  • Should there be an optional cloud mode for older phones, and how do we keep it private?
  • Business model: free with sponsored LGU or campus packs, a premium region bundle, or non-profit?
