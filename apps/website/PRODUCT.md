# Pusang Gala website

Register: brand. This is the Android app's public landing surface, not the app itself.

Users: NCR commuters, students and people planning unfamiliar supported journeys.
They want to understand the app, explore its interaction style and get the Android
release when a verified public APK is available. No sign-in or email capture is needed.

Brand voice: warm, curious, companionable. Preserve the app's Gaile cat mascot,
Sunset Playful colors, Nunito headings, DM Sans text and friendly English/Taglish.
The reference is the current mobile app and its reviewed playful chat design in
docs/ux-redesign.md. Avoid corporate SaaS templates, fake reviews or fake download counts.

Purpose: introduce Gaile, explain supported NCR route information and offline local
capabilities, offer a clearly labeled interactive example and an honest installation
path. The sample is not live routing, and unconnected map/GPS/persistence must not be
advertised as finished. The local assistant requires one-time model setup; ordinary
route replies currently use locally phrased retrieved facts.

Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus, minimum 44px
targets, screen-reader announcements for changing demo content, readable mobile
layouts, reduced-motion support and usable core content without JavaScript.

Strategic principles: make the mascot and hands-on preview the identity; keep the
download status truthful; prioritize quick loading using local assets and no trackers.
